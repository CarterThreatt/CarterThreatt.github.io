Our Shared Vision

Where Marlie’s Light Takes the Stage

  ✦  

Marlie loved to dance, and she had a rare gift for making everyone around her feel like they belonged. Now that gift has a home. The Marlie Dru Sullivan Foundation and Marlie Center are proud to work hand in hand to bring a first-class performing arts center to the heart of downtown Rock Hill, a place where our community can come together, celebrate the arts, and make memories for generations to come.

Bearing Marlie’s name, the Center will welcome dancers, musicians, actors, students, and audiences of every age to a stage worthy of the talent in our region. It is the most meaningful way we know to Live Like Marlie, and it is the heart of everything the Foundation does.
