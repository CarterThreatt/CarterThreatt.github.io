Your Table & Tickets

✦ Your table

8 tickets · a full table

Two seats are for you and your spouse. The other six are among the most valuable fundraising tools we can give you.

A table is a $10,000 Platinum sponsorship. Our strong hope is that yours raises at least $10,000 toward your goal.

✦ Ways to fill it
  • Sell seats individually
  • Find a sponsor to underwrite the table
  • Invite guests who will give generously

A complimentary seat is a gracious thank-you for a major supporter. We ask that each seat be filled with intention; these seats raise support for Marlie’s mission, not free tickets for family and friends.

Allocate your tickets by January 15, 2027. For any ticket questions, text Stacie (843-754-7135) and Carter (803-554-0556).

Keep it fair & friendly

Rules & Policies

  • Two mirror balls: most money raised and most judges’ points at the Gala.
  • Cash and checks count once they’re received and verified by the Foundation.
  • Sponsorships credited to you: enter them yourself as a check, with Sponsorship as the check number.
  • Previous years’ sponsors cannot count toward your total unless the sponsor is your own company or family.
  • Fill your table with intention, and allocate your tickets by January 15.
  • The internal leaderboard link is for Stars only. Please don’t share it or post screenshots.
  • You provide your own shoes and costume.
