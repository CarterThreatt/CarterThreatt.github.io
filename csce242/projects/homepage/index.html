How you should actually plan your DC trip

  1. 1

    Pick Your Dates

    Choose when you will visit the nation’s Capitol. Ideally you should start planning no less than 5 months in advance.

  2. 2

    Send the request

    Reach out to your Representative or Senator as far out as possible, but no less than 90 days prior to your trip. Some tours can be scheduled in less than 90 days, but 3 months is best!

  3. 3

    Check your email

    Many top destinations in DC require background screening to visit. These these details are almost entirely requested through email. A missed email can lead to a missed tour!

  4. 4

    Show up prepared

    Bring a Real ID or passport. Many DC attractions now require them for entry! Pack and wear comfortable shoes, when walking between tours and attractions your feet will thank you!
