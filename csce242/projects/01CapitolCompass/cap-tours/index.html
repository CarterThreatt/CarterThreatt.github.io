Two different tours, two different processes. Most visitors request the wrong one — here's how to pick and how to ask.

How to request a staff-led tour

  1. 1

    Find your member's D.C. office

    House.gov and Senate.gov both have a "find your representative" lookup. Use the Washington office, not the district one.

    Lookup House Member Lookup Senator

  2. 2

    Send one request email

    Preferred date, two backup dates, group size, number of guest, number of children.

  3. 3

    Confirm the day before

    Most staff-led tours start inside the Member's office in a House Office Building, not on the Capitol steps.
