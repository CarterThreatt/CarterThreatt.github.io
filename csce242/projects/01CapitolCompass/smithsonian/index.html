Smithsonian Museums

Every Smithsonian museum in D.C. is free, but three of them still need a pass. Here is which ones, when to book, and what to expect at the door.

Free

Admission to every Smithsonian museum in D.C. is free. Only Cooper Hewitt in New York charges.

3 Passes

Air and Space, African American History and Culture, and the National Zoo need a free pass for every visitor, kids included.

10-5:30

Most museums are open every day from 10 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and close only on December 25.

Screening

Expect a bag check at the door, and metal detectors at the big museums on the Mall.

Which museums need a pass?

How to get your passes

Passes are free, but they run out

  1. 1 ABOUT 2 MONTHS OUT

    Air and Space

    Passes are released on the first of each month at noon Eastern, about two months ahead. Reserve as soon as your dates open.

    Reserve Passes

  2. 2 30 DAYS OUT

    African American History and Culture

    Advance passes release 30 days ahead. More are released online at 8:15 a.m. Eastern each day for same-day visits.

    Reserve Passes

  3. 3 ANYTIME BEFORE

    National Zoo

    Entry passes are free and required for everyone. Parking is paid, and it costs more on the day than if you buy it in advance.

    Reserve Passes

  4. 4 THE DAY OF

    Arrive on time

    Your pass is scanned at the door. Do not arrive before the entry time printed on it, and plan for a line at security.

Before you go

Every museum screens bags at the door

BRING

  • Your timed pass, if the museum needs one
  • A small bag. Bags are hand-checked or X-rayed, so less is faster.
  • A military ID if you served. African American History and Culture admits veterans and active duty without a pass.

DON'T BRING

  • Firearms
  • Knives, scissors, screwdrivers and other sharp objects
  • Pepper spray or Mace
  • Signs, placards or banners
