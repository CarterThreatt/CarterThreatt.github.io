Capitol Compass

Capitol Compass is a guide to visiting Washington, D.C. for people who have never been. Most travel sites list the monuments but skip the part visitors actually struggle with, which is how to get inside the buildings once they arrive. Touring the Capitol and touring the White House are two completely separate processes with different timelines, different request forms, and different security rules. This site walks through both of them in plain language so a first-time visitor knows what to do before booking a trip.

The second half of the site is written for new congressional interns. Every semester a new group arrives on Capitol Hill with no idea how the office buildings connect, what the dress expectations are, or where to find lunch in under twenty minutes. A short orientation would save most of them a week of guessing. Capitol Compass collects that practical information in one place alongside a photo gallery of the monuments and memorials across the National Mall.

What this Site Covers

  • How to tour the Capitol
  • How to tour the White House
  • How to get around the National Mall
  • Where to eat lunch on Capitol Hill
  • Where to find the best views of the monuments and memorials
